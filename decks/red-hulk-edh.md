# Red Hulk — Commander (EDH)

**Commander:** Red Hulk
**Colors:** Mono-red
**Power target:** Mid-power, budget-aware (no reserved-list cards, no $100 singles)
**Plan:** Hurt your own commander on purpose. Every point of damage he takes makes him bigger and fires a shot at something else.

---

## Commander

**Red Hulk** — {4}{R}{R} — Legendary Creature — Gamma Berserker Villain — 6/7
Reach, trample.
*Enrage* — Whenever Red Hulk is dealt damage, put a +1/+1 counter on him. When you do, he deals damage equal to the number of +1/+1 counters on him to any other target.

Source: Marvel Super Heroes (MSH) #149. Card text confirmed against published set data, not from memory.

The critical reading: the Enrage payoff scales off **total counters on him**, not the damage that triggered it. One point of damage from a 1/1 pinger on turn 8 can be a 9-damage shot. Damage events matter, damage *size* does not. Ten pings of 1 are worth vastly more than one hit for 10.

## How it actually wins

**Primary line — the machine gun.** Resolve Red Hulk, then get any repeatable damage source online. Pyrohemia is the best one: {R}, one damage to each creature and each player, once per activation, forever. Each activation is a separate damage event, so each one adds a counter and fires a shot. By the third or fourth activation you are shooting for 3, then 4, then 5, at any target you like. Point them at faces.

**Goblin Sharpshooter is the engine multiplier.** It untaps whenever *any* creature dies. Pair it with Pyrohemia and a table full of small creatures and it untaps repeatedly in a single turn, each untap being another ping into Red Hulk, each ping being a larger shot.

**Boros Reckoner doubles every ping.** Damage the Reckoner, it deals that much to any target — aim it at Red Hulk. One Pyrohemia activation now produces two Enrage triggers.

**Secondary line — Chandra's Ignition.** With a Red Hulk at 12 or more power, Ignition deals that much to each other creature and each opponent. It is a board wipe and roughly a third of everyone's life total in one card. With Repercussion out it is usually lethal.

**Backup line — commander damage.** He is a 6/7 with trample that grows every time anyone damages him. Embercleave or Gratuitous Violence turns two connections into 21.

## Mulligan guide

Keep hands with **four-plus lands and at least one of**: Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Ruby Medallion, or a two-mana rock. He costs six; you want him down on turn four or five, not seven.

Also strong to keep: any hand containing Pyrohemia plus enough lands to cast it and activate it twice.

Ship hands that are all payoff and no mana. This deck has a real curve and a six-drop commander — greed loses games here.

## Known traps and interactions

**Do not run Lightning Greaves.** Shroud stops *your own* pingers from targeting Red Hulk, which turns off the entire deck. Swiftfoot Boots grants hexproof, which you can still target through. Same reason Whispersilk Cloak is out.

**Pyrohemia hits you too.** One damage to each player every activation is real. In a long game you will pay 10-15 life to win. Track it. If you are the archenemy at 12 life, the engine is no longer free.

**Blasphemous Act kills Red Hulk.** Thirteen damage to each creature. He triggers Enrage first and gets one final shot off, then dies. That is an acceptable trade for a full wipe, but cast it on purpose, not on autopilot.

**Arcbond is symmetric and dangerous.** Cast on Red Hulk, every damage event to him makes him deal that much to each other creature *and each player* — you included. It is a finisher, not a value play.

**Dictate of the Twin Gods and Furnace-style doublers cut both ways.** They double the damage Red Hulk deals, which is the point. They also double the damage Pyrohemia deals to you. Deploy them when you are converting, not when you are grinding.

**Enrage counts events, not damage.** A doubled 1-damage ping is still one Enrage trigger. Amplifiers make his *shots* bigger; they do not make him grow faster.

## Upgrade path (when budget opens up)

- **Fiery Emancipation** — triples Red Hulk's damage. The single largest power increase available.
- **Deflecting Swat** — free redirect, protects the commander and steals removal.
- **Nykthos, Shrine to Nyx** — mono-red devotion ramp, real acceleration for a six-drop commander.
- **Wheel of Fortune** — strict upgrade over Magus of the Wheel.
- **Terror of the Peaks** — every creature that enters is another ping you can aim at Red Hulk.
- **City on Fire** — a second tripler if you want to go all the way over the top.
- **Ancient Copper Dragon** — the best pure ramp payoff in mono-red.

Cuts for these, in order: Fire Servant, Combustible Gearhulk, Magus of the Wheel, Wayfarer's Bauble.
